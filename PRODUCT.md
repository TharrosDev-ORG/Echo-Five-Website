# Echo-Five Consulting — Product / Brand Context

register: brand

## Product purpose

A one-page marketing site for **Echo-Five Consulting**, the solo practice of **Mark Abdelnour**: change management and Microsoft 365 adoption for Canadian public-sector and government organizations. The site exists to earn a first meeting. A program lead should be able to read the hero and pitch the firm in one sentence, then trust it enough to send a note.

The design IS the product here. A visitor's impression in the first fold is the thing being made.

## Users

- **Primary:** federal / provincial / municipal program leads, transformation directors, and IT managers evaluating whether to hand a department-wide rollout to an independent consultant.
- **Context:** read on an office laptop under fluorescent light, often while comparing vendors. They are risk-averse, procurement-literate, and allergic to hype.
- **What earns trust:** structure, precision, plain language, a real named person who does the work end-to-end, no buzzword fog.

## Brand voice

Three words: **precise · credible · human (not corporate)**.

- Plain, declarative sentences. No consulting-speak, no "synergy / leverage / journey".
- Confidence through specificity ("Teams, SharePoint, Purview", "Prosci-aligned", "twenty years") not adjectives.
- The principal speaks in the first person where it counts. This is a person, not a faceless firm.

## Aesthetic direction

**Govtech precision — Swiss / International Typographic Style.** Named reference: **Müller-Brockmann grid posters** (black + cool-white + one committed signal-red). A strict, *visible* modular grid is the voice. Big confident grotesque type. One saturated red used structurally, not decoratively. Custom abstract "echo / signal" visuals; no photography.

## Anti-references (do NOT look like these)

- **The old Echo-Five site** — editorial-serif AI lane: Fraunces display serif, tiny uppercase tracked label above every section heading, italic markers, hairline rules everywhere, beige + slate-navy, zero imagery. This is exactly what we are escaping.
- **GOV.UK clone** — the literal government-blue + Transport-font cosplay. We want govtech *rigor*, not govtech *costume*.
- **Generic SaaS** — gradient hero, three identical icon cards, rounded-corner everything, navy-and-gold fintech.

## Strategic principles

- Commit to the red. A Committed color strategy: signal-red carries real surface, not a timid ≤10% accent.
- The grid is the brand. Show the keylines; let position and scale carry hierarchy instead of label kickers.
- Every word earns its place. Keep the strong existing lines ("Change that lands. Tools that get used."; "twenty years, one job, done properly").
- Accessibility is credibility for a public-sector audience: WCAG AA is a floor, not a goal.
