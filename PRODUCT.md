# Echofive Solutions: Product / Brand Context

register: brand

## Product purpose

A six-page marketing site for **Echofive Solutions Inc.**, a senior change management
practice specializing in Microsoft 365 adoption for Canadian public-sector and
enterprise organizations, and for its product, **Advanta365**. The site exists to earn a
first meeting. A program lead
should be able to read the hero, understand exactly what the firm does, and trust it
enough to send a note.

The design IS the product here. A visitor's impression in the first fold is the thing
being made.

## Users

- **Primary:** federal, provincial, and municipal program leads, transformation
  directors, and IT managers evaluating who should run a department-wide Microsoft 365
  rollout, plus enterprise and Crown-corporation equivalents.
- **Context:** read on an office laptop under fluorescent light, often while comparing
  vendors. Risk-averse, procurement-literate, allergic to hype.
- **What earns trust:** structure, precision, plain language, a named client roster,
  real credentials and clearance, no buzzword fog.

## Positioning

Echofive presents as a **firm**, not a lone consultant. Voice is first-person plural
("we", "our work"). The reader should sense a small, senior team (a handful of
practitioners) without any headcount ever being stated. Founded and led by Mark
Abdelnour; the site never centers a single person.

## Brand voice

Three words: **precise · credible · human (not corporate)**.

- Plain, declarative sentences. No consulting-speak: no "synergy", "leverage",
  "journey", "transformation theatre".
- Confidence through specificity ("Teams, SharePoint Online, Copilot", "Prosci ADKAR",
  "30+ years", "Secret Level II") rather than adjectives.
- Keep the strong lines: "Change that lands. Tools that get used." and
  "Thirty years. One job. Done properly."
- **No em dashes** anywhere in body copy.

## Experience

30+ years in practice. Use "30+ years" or "more than thirty years" everywhere; never
"20+" or "twenty years".

## Aesthetic direction

**Signal.** Premium and conversion-focused, with a point of view: black and white
grounds for weight and reading, one electric blue for actions, key data, and a thin
"signal line" (from the Echofive name) that starts as the adoption curve and travels down
every page, becoming each page's key diagram. Giant typographic numerals carry the proof.
Crafted restraint: award-quality type and layout, CSS-only motion, never hiding content.

- **Color:** black (`#0A0A0B`) and white grounds, mist for alternate bands, and one blue
  (`#2F5BFF`). On black, small blue text uses a lighter blue (`#6E8CFF`) because the
  main blue is only 3.83:1 there. The closing call to action is a full blue band with
  solid white text. See `DESIGN.md`.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque headings, Geist text. Sentence-case headings, very large
  and tight for the home headline and the proof numerals ("30+", "II", "ADKAR", "365").
- **Structure:** a home page that leads with proof (30+ years, clearance, certifications,
  named clients), then why rollouts stall, services, the ADKAR model, the Advanta365
  band, and the talk. Inner pages for Services, Approach, Advanta365, About, and Contact.
  A clear "Start a conversation" path on every page, including a fixed bar on phones.
- **Motion:** reveal on scroll, a signal line that draws down the page, a headline that
  rises once on load, a slow client marquee. All progressive enhancement and all off
  under reduced motion; every page is fully readable without JavaScript.

## Anti-references (do NOT look like these)

- **Echo-Five "Midnight & Vermilion":** plain, editorial document layout with one
  vermilion accent. Credible but too plain to feel premium, and weak on conversion.
- **Echo-Five v1:** editorial-serif lane (Fraunces, tiny tracked uppercase kickers,
  hairlines everywhere, beige).
- **Echo-Five v2:** Swiss-grid govtech in navy-slate + teal.
- **Echo-Five (warm-amber rebuild):** warm paper + deep ink + signal amber, radar hero.
- **Echo-Five dark "Operations / Instrument":** graphite + signal green oscilloscope.
- **Echo-Five "Bone & Cobalt":** warm bone + cobalt, WebGL field, pinned scroll scenes.
  Visually strong, but long and effect-heavy; hard for busy buyers to scan. Signal stays
  distinct from it: blue on black and white rather than cobalt on bone, no WebGL, no
  pinned scenes.
- **GOV.UK clone:** government-blue + Transport-font cosplay. We want govtech rigor,
  not govtech costume.
- **Navy or gov-blue looks:** Signal's dark is black, never navy, and its blue is an
  electric accent rather than a ground (except the single closing band).
- **"Spruce & Citron" (the previous direction of this site):** deep spruce grounds with
  citron and moss accents. Premium, but generic: it read as a well-made template and
  lacked a signature. Signal replaces it with one memorable idea (the line) and giant
  numerals.
- **Generic SaaS / dev-tool slop:** purple gradients, three identical icon cards,
  rounded-everything, glowing-neon-on-black with no restraint.

## Strategic principles

- Lead with proof: clearance, certifications, years, and named clients belong in the
  first fold, not at the bottom.
- Every page ends with the ask.
- Specificity is the credibility engine: name the clients, name the tools, name the
  certs and the clearance.
- Accessibility is credibility for a public-sector audience: WCAG AA is the floor.
