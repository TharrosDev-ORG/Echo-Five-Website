## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? No.** It has a committed point of view: the callsign/echo/radar motif, amber-on-warm-ink "Committed" palette, editorial numbered lists instead of icon-card grids, a typographic client wall, a live canvas radar hero. It passes both slop tests; a viewer asks "how was the radar built?", not "which AI made this?"

**Deterministic scan (detect.mjs):** 1 finding — `em-dash-overuse` in `app/layout.tsx` (6 em-dashes). These are real: the dash in the page title, the title template, and the OpenGraph title. They violate the brand's own "no em dashes anywhere" rule and they surface in the browser tab and Google results. Everywhere else (all of `lib/content.ts`) is clean.

**Visual overlays:** Not available. This environment exposes headless Chrome screenshots only, not an automatable browser tab with console injection, so the live `detect.js` overlay flow was not run. Evidence for Assessment B is the CLI detector plus full-page desktop/mobile screenshots captured manually.

## Overall Impression

A genuinely well-built brand site, not a starting point. The hero lands, the palette has conviction, the design system is disciplined (tokens, reduced-motion fallbacks, skip link, AA-tuned muted colors, server-rendered copy). The single biggest opportunity is not visual — it is the conversion moment: the site is engineered to earn the trust of risk-averse, procurement-literate federal buyers, then routes them to a personal gmail address.

## What's Working

- **The motif is real, not decoration.** Radar hero, SectionMark channel coordinates, and ping rings all express one idea. `SectionMark` deliberately side-steps the eyebrow/numbered-scaffolding anti-pattern: plain-set label, tied to a named system.
- **Copy earns trust through specificity.** Named departments (DND, SSC, TBS), named certs (Prosci ADKAR, Secret Level II), real tools. No consulting-speak.
- **Committed color carries the brand.** Amber owns the motif, CTAs, and the full drenched contact close. Alternating ink/paper grounds give rhythm.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The conversion CTA routes to a Gmail address.** Every "Book a conversation" link is `mailto:Mark.Abdelnour@gmail.com`. For a Secret-Level-II-cleared, 20-year Government of Canada practice presenting as a firm, a personal gmail at conversion reads as "small unincorporated vendor" to exactly the procurement-literate buyer the site targets. Fix: use an address on the owned domain (`echo-five.ca`) in `lib/site.ts`. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

- **[P1] Mobile hero headline clips off-screen.** At 390px, `.t-display` floors at `2.7rem` and the headline is split with a hard `<br/>`, so "Tools that get used." runs past the ~350px content width. With global `overflow-x: hidden`, the text is cut off, not scrollable — content loss on the first fold. Fix: lower the clamp floor (~2.1–2.3rem) and/or drop the forced `<br/>` below `sm`. Re-test contact/about display headings at 360/390px. Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

- **[P2] "Method" nav label collides with the Method section.** Nav "Method" links to `#process`, while a section literally titled Method (Advanta365) sits at `#method` and is not in the nav. Services is also omitted. Fix: rename the nav item or realign anchors. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

- **[P2] Em-dashes in metadata titles.** `app/layout.tsx` uses the dash in the title, template, and OG title — a self-inflicted breach of the "no em dashes" rule, visible in the tab and SERP. Fix: replace with a colon or the brand mid-dot. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

- **[P3] Misleading hover affordance + minor DRY.** Client-wall cards animate on hover but are non-interactive `cursor: default`. Contact reimplements SectionMark inline. Fix: drop hover shift on non-links; extract a dark SectionMark variant. Suggested command: /impeccable polish

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (mobile):** first thing at 390px is a headline sliced off at the screen edge — reads as "broken" before any copy.

**Riley (stress tester):** taps "Book a conversation" with no mail client and nothing happens; no copy fallback. Hovers a client card, sees it light up, clicks, nothing.

**Dana (federal program lead, project persona):** impressed by clearances and tenure, clicks contact, sees `@gmail.com`, quietly downgrades the firm.

## Minor Observations

- Reveal content is correctly gated behind a `useReducedMotion` static fallback and is server-rendered; the blank headless captures are an artifact, not a bug.
- Two huge `t-display` headings (About, Contact) beyond the hero — fine, spaced far apart.
- Confirm `text-balance` + clamp interplay at tablet widths once the floor changes.

## Questions to Consider

- What happens if the only contact channel (mailto) silently fails? Is a copyable address or tiny form worth it?
- The nav exposes 5 of 8 sections. Deliberate editing, or are Services/Method hidden by accident?
- If the hero headline shrinks on mobile anyway, is the forced two-line `<br/>` earning its place?
